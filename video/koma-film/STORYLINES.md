# Creative exploration

Five devices: a queue of unsent thoughts; a parking machine thanked by accident; a wandering comma collecting stories; a radio dial of moods; a paper receipt itemising today's inner monologue.

Selected — **Isi kepala bukan satu genre.** A paper browser full of incongruous thoughts becomes Koma's genuine paper interface. One embarrassing parking-machine thought carries through an anonymous post, a reply, and the final invitation. The first two seconds are a giant editorial question interrupted by handwritten-looking cutout tabs. Form: object-to-object chain with a tempo break. Ground: actual Koma paper, Georgia and Courier, violet ink, authentic collage. Sound: Indonesian male voice, playful library groove, physical editing accents.

Alternative — **Sama-sama, mesin parkir.** A tiny everyday social mishap is treated like a serious documentary, then answered by another anonymous person. First two seconds: the words SAMA-SAMA stamped across a parking ticket. Form: deadpan locked-off tableaux with increasingly intrusive replies. Sound: sparse comedic pauses and paper foley.

Alternative — **Siaran dari kepala.** A radio dial abruptly changes from absurd question to sincere story and lands on Koma. First two seconds: three interrupted sentence fragments. Form: continuous movement across a tactile dial, which becomes the Koma comma. Sound: channel cuts resolving into a single warm track.

The full-file commission authorizes proceeding with the selected concept without a selection stop.
